# Ways to close a gap

Each gap needs two things: a way to **learn** the skill and a way to **prove** it. The best options do both at once.

## Where to grow

**Inside the current job** (usually the best place to start: no cost, real results, visible to the people who decide on promotions):

- Ask for a stretch task or a small project that uses the missing skill.
- Take over a responsibility nobody owns yet (a process, a tool, onboarding new colleagues, a report).
- Start an improvement: fix something slow, costly, or error-prone, and measure before and after.
- Shadow or pair with someone strong in the skill; cover for them when they're away.
- Cross-train in a neighbouring team or department.
- Ask the manager directly: "I want to grow into <target>. What would I need to show you?" Their answer is market data too.

**Outside the job:**

- Courses: online, evening classes, university short programs.
- Certificates and licenses from recognized bodies.
- Books and self-study with practice.
- Mentoring: a mentor from a professional association, a former manager, or someone already in the target role.
- Communities and professional associations: meetups, working groups, committees.
- Volunteering or pro bono work using the skill (NGOs, community organizations, school boards).
- Small paid side work or freelance jobs.
- Personal projects that solve a realistic problem (not a tutorial copy).

## Ways to prove

| Proof strength | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Certificate or license from a recognized body, degree, award, peer-reviewed publication, official registration |
| **Shown** | A result at work with numbers, a portfolio piece or case study, a public project, a talk or workshop given, an article or post, teaching or training others, a written reference or recommendation from a manager or client |
| **Claimed** | A line on the CV or LinkedIn with nothing behind it |

Move gaps up this table. When job ads name a specific certificate or license, that is the proof to aim for.

## Proof-gap quick wins

When the user has the skill but can't prove it:

- Dig out the numbers: before/after, volume, time or money saved, quality scores, feedback. Write the result as a CV highlight.
- Write a short case study of real work (anonymized if needed) for a portfolio, LinkedIn, or a personal site.
- Ask a manager, colleague, or client for a written recommendation that names the skill.
- Take the certificate exam for a skill they already use every day.
- Present the work internally (a team session) or externally (a meetup, a professional association event).
- Put it where the market looks: update LinkedIn and the CV (the `cv-builder` and `presence-audit` skills help).

## Examples by field

These are starting points. Always check what the market for the user's goal actually asks for.

| Field | Common gaps | Typical ways to close and prove them |
|---|---|---|
| Healthcare | Specialty skills, advanced life support, leadership | Accredited specialty courses and certifications, rotating to a unit, precepting new staff, quality-improvement projects with measured outcomes |
| Education | Digital tools, special needs, leadership, curriculum design | Accredited training, leading a school project, mentoring new teachers, publishing lesson materials, measured student outcomes |
| Finance and accounting | Professional qualifications, reporting and analytics tools, regulation | Professional body qualifications (for example ACCA, CPA, CFA), automating a report at work with time saved, owning a process end to end |
| Sales and marketing | Analytics, specific platforms, bigger deals, strategy | Platform certifications, running a campaign or account with measured results, a case study, mentoring juniors |
| Design and creative | New tools, research methods, a specialty | Portfolio case studies showing process and results, redesign projects, talks, competitions |
| Engineering and trades | Licenses, new standards and software, supervision | Chamber or trade licenses, standard-specific courses, supervising a crew or project, documented project results |
| Software and data | Languages, cloud, architecture, leadership | Projects at work with measured results, public projects with a good README, cloud certifications, talks, open-source contributions |
| Operations and admin | Process improvement, tools, project management | Lean or project management certificates, an improvement project with before/after numbers, owning a tool rollout |
| Research and academia | Methods, funding, publications, teaching | Collaborations, method courses, grant applications, papers, conference talks, teaching or supervising |
| Freelance and independent | Clients, reputation, pricing | Portfolio, testimonials, a niche specialty, platform ratings, published expertise |

## How to choose

For each gap, prefer the option that is:

1. **What the market asks for.** A certificate that appears in 8 of 12 ads beats a nicer course that appears in none.
2. **Learning and proof at once.** A work project with a measurable result beats a course alone.
3. **Within the user's constraints.** Hours, budget, and learning style from `about.md`.
4. **Connected to their edge.** Building on a strength makes the user stand out, not just qualify.
